Arithmetic Reasoning

A recruit runs 3 miles in 24 minutes. At the same pace, how many minutes will it take to run 5 miles?

  1. 32 minutes
  2. 36 minutes
  3. 40 minutes
  4. 45 minutes
Show the answer and explanation

C. 40 minutes. First find the pace: 24 minutes ÷ 3 miles = 8 minutes per mile. Then apply it: 8 minutes × 5 miles = 40 minutes. Every practice question on this site comes with a step-by-step explanation like this one.
