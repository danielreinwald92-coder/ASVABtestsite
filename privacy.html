PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

The short version

Mission ASVAB is a free practice site run by one person. You can take every practice test without an account. We never sell your personal information. To keep the site free, some pages show ads from Google, and Google may use cookies to choose those ads.

What we store

Without an account: your test progress, results, study missions and flashcard progress are saved only in your own browser (local storage). They stay on your device and you can clear them anytime by clearing your browser data.

With an account: if you sign up, we store your email address, the optional profile details you enter (name, age, education, ZIP code and test date), your test results and your study progress. This lets you see your history on any device. Accounts are hosted by Supabase, our database provider.

You can delete your account and all of its data at any time from your dashboard.

Analytics

We use privacy-friendly, cookieless web analytics from our hosting provider to count page visits (for example, how many people opened a page and roughly which country they came from). These tools do not use cookies and do not identify you personally.

Advertising

Some pages show a banner ad served by Google AdSense. Ads are never shown during a practice test. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites, and to measure how ads perform.

You can turn off personalized ads in Google Ad Settings, and learn more at how Google uses information from sites that use its services. You can also opt out of many third-party ad cookies at aboutads.info. Visitors in some regions, such as the European Economic Area and the UK, will be asked for consent before personalized ads are shown.

Children

Mission ASVAB is intended for people preparing to join the military, who are generally 17 or older. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page. Questions about your data can be sent through the report or contact options on the site.
